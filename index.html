Play Ludo with a friend on another phone

Roll online, talk on voice chat, and listen to the same song while you play.

Download for Android

Version 1.0. Needs Android 5.0 or newer.

Tap the dice

Made for playing with friends

Play online

Create a room, send your friend a short code on WhatsApp, and you are in. No sign-up and no account.

Talk while you play

Voice chat is built in. Your mic starts muted, so nobody hears you by accident. Tap it when you want to talk.

Play a song together

Pick a song from your phone and it plays on both phones at the same time. Whoever started it controls it, and your friend can mute it. Songs up to 30 MB.

Team up, 2 vs 2

Team Up mode puts you and a partner against two others. When all your tokens are home, you keep rolling and move your partner's tokens.

No friend around? Play against the computer, or pass one phone around.

Install the app

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  4. Open Ludo and tap Play Online.

Connect with a friend

  1. Host: tap Create Room and send the code to your friend on WhatsApp.
  2. Friend: tap Join Room, paste the code, and send the reply code back.
  3. Host: paste the reply code. The game starts.

If the internet drops mid-game, the game pauses and waits up to 45 seconds for your friend to come back.
